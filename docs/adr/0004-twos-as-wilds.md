# Twos as wild cards

All twos in the shoe are wild, subject to the same naming and adjacency rules as jokers. They form one wild family: a two cannot sit next to a joker in a meld.

Using rank-as-wild is unusual compared to joker-only or ace-as-wild variants. Treating twos as naturals in some contexts (for example Ace-low runs) requires explicit rules elsewhere; twos played as substitutes follow the wild rules, not natural rank rules.
