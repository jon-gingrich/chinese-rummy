# Lay-off only after opening

Once a player completes their opening turn, they may not create new melds for the rest of that round. From the following turn onward, they may only lay off on existing melds — their own or other players'.

The opening turn itself is contract-only: no lay-offs on that turn. This two-phase structure (open, then lay off) is core to how the table plays and differs from open melding throughout the round.
