# Chinese Rummy

Family house-rules contract rummy played with two decks, jokers, and wild twos. Ten rounds per game; lowest cumulative deadwood score wins.

## The game

**Game**:
A full play-through of all ten contract rounds between the same seated players.
_Avoid_: Match, session (when meaning the whole ten-round game)

**Round**:
One deal from fresh shuffle through to someone going out. Each round has a specific opening contract.
_Avoid_: Hand (when meaning the round), deal (when meaning the round)

**Hand**:
The cards held privately by one player during a round, before they are played or discarded.
_Avoid_: Deal (when meaning a player's cards)

**Shoe**:
The full pack before dealing: two standard 52-card decks plus four jokers (108 cards total).
_Avoid_: Deck (when meaning the whole shoe), pack

**Stock**:
The face-down draw pile during a round.
_Avoid_: Deck, draw pile

**Discard pile**:
The face-up pile where each turn ends. Only the top card may be taken.
_Avoid_: Waste pile, bin

## Melds and cards

**Meld**:
A valid set or run laid on the table.
_Avoid_: Combination, group

**Set**:
Three or more cards of the same rank. Duplicate suits are allowed (two-deck game).
_Avoid_: Group, book (unless your table already uses book consistently)

**Run**:
Three or more cards of the same suit in consecutive rank order. Ace may be high or low; K-A-2 wraparound is not allowed.
_Avoid_: Sequence, straight

**Natural**:
A card played for its printed rank and suit — not standing in for another card.
_Avoid_: Real card, concrete card

**Wild**:
A joker or a two played as a substitute for another rank. The player must declare what rank a wild represents when it is first played in a meld.
_Avoid_: Wildcard (fine in prose, but **Wild** is the canonical term)

**Wild adjacency**:
Wilds may not occupy adjacent slots in a meld. No joker-next-to-joker, no two-next-to-two, and no joker-next-to-two.
_Avoid_: Touching wilds, wild touching rule

**Wild relocation**:
When a natural card replaces a wild during lay-off, the displaced wild must move to another set or run on the same player's board. The relocating player chooses the destination. Adjacency rules do not apply during relocation.
_Avoid_: Wild swap, pulling a wild

## Contracts and play phases

**Contract**:
The exact melds a player must lay in a single opening turn for the current round. Meld sizes must match the contract precisely — not merely meet a minimum.
_Avoid_: Requirement, quota, phase goal

**Contract schedule**:
The fixed sequence of contracts across the ten rounds of a game:

| Round | Contract |
| ----- | -------- |
| 1 | 2 sets of 3 |
| 2 | 2 runs of 3 |
| 3 | 1 set of 3, 1 run of 4 |
| 4 | 2 sets of 4 |
| 5 | 2 runs of 4 |
| 6 | 1 set of 4, 1 run of 5 |
| 7 | 2 sets of 5 |
| 8 | 2 runs of 5 |
| 9 | 1 set of 3, 1 run of 7 |
| 10 | 2 sets of 3, 1 run of 7 |

**Opening turn**:
The first turn on which a player lays their contract melds. All required contract melds must go down together in this turn.
_Avoid_: Lay-down turn, going in, meld-in

**Opened**:
A player who has completed their opening turn for the current round.
_Avoid_: In, down, melded in

**Lay off**:
Adding a card to an existing meld on the table after your opening turn. Includes wild replacement with relocation.
_Avoid_: Add on, extend, hit

**Going out**:
Ending the round by discarding your last card, leaving zero cards in hand.
_Avoid_: Winning the hand, emptying (going out requires a final discard)

## Roles and turn order

**Dealer**:
The player who deals the current round. Rotates clockwise each round.
_Avoid_: Banker

**Lead player**:
The player who takes the first turn of the round — seated immediately left of the dealer.
_Avoid_: First player, opener

## Scoring

**Deadwood**:
Unmelded cards remaining in a player's hand when someone goes out.
_Avoid_: Points in hand, leftover cards

**Go out**:
The player who ends the round by discarding their last card. They score zero deadwood for that round.
_Avoid_: Winner of the round (scoring term — they went out, but the game winner is lowest total after round 10)

**Deadwood scoring**:
Point values for unmelded cards when someone goes out: Ace 15; King, Queen, Jack, Ten 10; Three through Nine 5; Two and Joker 20 each. Lowest cumulative total after round 10 wins the game.
_Avoid_: Penalty points, card values (too vague)

## Online play

**Room**:
A persistent online space where players gather before and during a game. Identified by a short code.
_Avoid_: Lobby (when meaning the room itself), table

**Host**:
The player who created the room and may start the game once enough players are seated and ready.
_Avoid_: Owner, admin

**Seat**:
One of two to five player positions in a room.
_Avoid_: Slot, chair

**Ready**:
A player's signal that they are prepared for the host to start the game.
_Avoid_: Locked in, set
